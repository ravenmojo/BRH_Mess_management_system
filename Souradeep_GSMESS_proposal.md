# <span style="color: #841818ff;">A Vision for a Transparent, Fair, and High-Quality BRH Mess</span>

**Candidate:** Souradeep
**Position:** General Secretary of Mess, B.R. Ambedkar Hall of Residence

---

## <span style="color: #1d3557;">The Core Philosophy: Your Mess, Your Rights</span>

With over 1,600 boarders from all over India, B.R. Ambedkar Hall is very diverse, and our mess food should reflect that. For too long, we have faced bad food quality, hygiene issues, and a system where you lose money if you don't eat.

Every rupee you pay must be seen in the food on your plate. As your General Secretary of Mess, my focus will be on <strong style="color: #d62828;">Strict Accountability</strong>, <strong style="color: #2a9d8f;">Good Food Quality</strong>, and <strong style="color: #e76f51;">Financial Fairness</strong>. We will move from the old paper system to a modern, digital mess system where your complaints are heard instantly, your money is saved, and nothing is hidden from you.

---

## <span style="color: #1d3557;">Key Proposals & Action Plan</span>

### <span style="color: #457b9d;">1. Bringing Real Transparency and Accountability</span>
The days of writing complaints in old registers and not getting answers are over. We will use the **[<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/4/41/Flag_of_India.svg" alt="India Flag" width="20" style="vertical-align: middle; display: inline-block; margin-right: 4px;"> BROS (B.R. Ambedkar Hall Mess Management) App](https://bros.dpdns.org)** to make everything open and clear. This app is completely **open-source and free for everyone**.
- **Solve Complaints Instantly:** Found a hygiene issue? Is the food bad today? Just take a picture and upload it to the app's Grievance portal.
- **Public Tracking:** Every complaint will be tracked online. You will see exactly when it is solved, who solved it, and what action was taken. By keeping open records, we <strong style="color: #d62828;">make sure that we know exactly what is being served to us as food.</strong>
- **Easy Mess Duty:** The app will make mess duties very easy for boarders. You can quickly log your checks, check the raw materials, and demand your rights without any hassle.

### <span style="color: #2a9d8f;">2. Better Vegetarian Food & High-Quality Raw Materials</span>
A big problem in our hall is the quality of vegetarian food. We will improve this without affecting the non-vegetarian food.
- **Strict Checks on Raw Materials:** I will use the app to regularly check the raw materials. We will make sure that the good quality ingredients we pay for are exactly what is used in the kitchen, <strong style="color: #d62828;">stopping the vendors from replacing them with inferior or bad quality items</strong> as has been the case in the past.
- **Focus on Veg Dishes:** We will put special focus on the taste and variety of vegetarian dishes. We will ensure they are just as good as the best items on the menu, respecting the needs of many boarders in our hall.

### <span style="color: #e76f51;">3. Extra Food Options on Payment Basis</span>
Boarders deserve more choices beyond the normal menu.
- **More Choices:** I will introduce a variety of extra items (like special paneer dishes, specific chicken items, desserts, or regional food).
- **Fair Prices:** All extra items will be sold strictly according to the official HMC (Hall Management Centre) prices—<strong style="color: #e76f51;">no extra or unfair charges.</strong>

### <span style="color: #8b5cf6;">4. Financial Fairness: The Smart Mess Rebate System</span>
It is unfair that boarders pay for meals they do not eat. 
- **The Solution:** I will put in place a digital tracking system to check who is eating Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner. 
- **The Guarantee:** <strong style="color: #2a9d8f;">If you don't eat, you don't pay.</strong> You will get your money back (mess rebate) for the meals you miss, making sure you only pay for what you actually eat.
